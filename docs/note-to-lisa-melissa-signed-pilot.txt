HOW READ-ONLY ACTUALLY WORKS IN SKY - please read this part

This is the one thing I want to be straight about, because it works differently than you'd expect.

SKY API has no "read-only" permission you can hand out.  There is no scope to check, no box to tick.  A SKY access token inherits the permissions of whoever authorizes the connection.  If a full administrator clicks approve, the token can do everything that administrator can do - regardless of what our app intends.

So read-only isn't something I can promise you with a setting.  It comes from WHO does the authorizing.

What that means practically:

- Our commitment: AthleticOS issues GET requests only during the pilot.  No POST, no PATCH, no DELETE.  Every call is logged with timestamp, endpoint, and response code, and I'll hand you that log any time you ask.
- Your structural guarantee: have the OAuth authorization done by an account whose permissions are limited to reading athletics and calendar data.  Then the token is incapable of writing, no matter what.

I'd rather you have the second one than take my word on the first.  If you'd like to create a limited service account for this, that's the cleanest path and I'm happy to work from whatever it can see.
