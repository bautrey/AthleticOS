Why this matters: NWS gives us a forecast wet bulb globe temperature, which is
the right measure and the right lead time for planning. It is not the on-site
reading a heat policy is administered from. If TCA already has a station on the
fields, we read from it for the day-of number and keep the forecast for the
Tuesday warning. The code is already built behind an interface so a station feed
drops in without a rewrite.

limits do not apply and TAPPS publishes theirs as recommended. We deliberately
ship with no number in the box - whatever TCA's own plan says is what goes in.
